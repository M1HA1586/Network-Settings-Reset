## ✅ Before you ask

1. Use the **latest** [`NSR.bat`](https://github.com/M1HA1586/Network-Settings-Reset/releases/latest/download/NSR.bat).
2. Run **Network Diagnostics** (`4` on the main menu). It tells you where the problem is.
3. Run **Network Toolbox**, then **Network Report** (`7`, then `8`). It saves everything in one text file on your Desktop. Remove private data before you share it.

NSR is free and made by one person, so replies can take a little time. Thank you for your patience. 💙
